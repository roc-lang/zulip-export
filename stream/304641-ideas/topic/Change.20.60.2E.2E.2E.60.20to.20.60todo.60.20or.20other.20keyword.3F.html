Topic: Change `...` to `todo` or other keyword?

notramo (Oct 01 2026 at 20:59):

I often use the ... keyword. However, due to sharing the dot with the method call syntax, in many places it's a parse error:

main! = |args| {
    _ = args
    ...
}

Putting it in parentheses helps:

main! = |args| {
    _ = args
    (...)
}

but its too complicated, compared to a todo:

main! = |args| {
    _ = args
    todo
}

notramo (Oct 01 2026 at 21:00):

Given that it's a construct that developers quickly use while writing the code, and not meant to be persisted, I think it would be simpler to use todo.

notramo (Oct 01 2026 at 21:01):

Another argument for todo: it's greppable, and shows up alongside the comment todos.

notramo (Oct 01 2026 at 21:02):

Also, quick in-editor searches for ... need actually a \.\.\., because some editors (including Helix and Flow) default to regex search on the / hotkey.

notramo (Oct 01 2026 at 21:03):

It's made worse by some keyboard layouts needing a modifier key for backslash.

notramo (Oct 01 2026 at 21:05):

For example, Hungarian layout requires AltGr+q, so to search for a ... it requires
AltGr+q . AltGr+q . AltGr+q .

notramo (Oct 01 2026 at 21:07):

9 keyboard strokes, of which 6 are combination pairs, which are slower because having to wait with the AltGr release until the q is pressed.

notramo (Oct 01 2026 at 21:07):

Compared to a todo keyword: 4 keystrokes, no combinations.

notramo (Oct 01 2026 at 21:10):

And even on the English layout, the dot and the backslash are both on the same hand (the two weakest fingers), compared to todo, which is alternated hands.

notramo (Oct 01 2026 at 21:11):

These matter a lot, since this keyword is mostly used when writing the code by hand.
