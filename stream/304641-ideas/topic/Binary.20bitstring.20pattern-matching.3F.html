Evan (Oct 16 2026 at 18:20):

imo (having written a lot of bitstring matches in erlang) it's nice for doing stuff like protocol framing which often has a lot of fiddly sub-byte things to switch on, so you can do a multiple clause match (usually at the function head level) that match on things like that.

That said, I do think that it's not the most discoverable thing as the /format trailer hides a lot of behavior that might better be exposed at a higher level (e.g. endianess, byte ordering, etc.).

I think an interesting middle-ground might be adding something like 0b010._...1 where . in a bitstring pattern means "don't care", so this would compile to something like `val & 0x20 != 0 && val & 0x01 != 0, which would give you the ability to switch on a few bits without the overhead of an additional sublanguage to have to handle.
