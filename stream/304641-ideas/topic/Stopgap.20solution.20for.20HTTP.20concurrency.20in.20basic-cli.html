Topic: Stopgap solution for HTTP concurrency in basic-cli

Karakatiza (Nov 11 2026 at 22:25):

While the concurrency in Roc remains out of scope for now, I'd like to close a surface gap in the basic-cli UX - that waiting for a HTTP request to resolve blocks the execution of a program. I have CLI app use-case where I need to do stuff while the request resolves. I vibe-coded what I feel is a reasonable low-tech solution for this that doesn't involve designing a complete concurrency system - in https://github.com/roc-lang/basic-cli/pull/553 . I'd love feedback on this - I see this as a simple, unintrusive, practical stopgap solution until a more general solution arrives - either for Roc or just basic-cli.

I mean, I 'd love to come up with a more general solution, but I imagine it would take a lot of time and deliberation to arrive to and ultimately merge, and time is not on my side with this use case.
