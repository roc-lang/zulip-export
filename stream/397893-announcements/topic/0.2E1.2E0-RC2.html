Richard Feldman (Nov 25 2026 at 22:09):

building on #announcements > Release candidate: Roc 0.1.0-RC1 we now have an 0.1.0-RC2 release candidate published!

The commit is ee835686f9eae076bee7422c26e7cf8deb20aa0e - nearly the same commit as RC1, except with a fix for a roc check hang cherry-picked onto it because it was blocking a project and couldn't be worked around.

I am being intentionally conservative about the RC process because I want to avoid accidentally introducing regressions. (There have been a bunch of performance improvements and bugfixes landed on main which I am intentionally leaving out of RC2 to avoid the chance of regressions. There will be an 0.1.1 with them released after 0.1.0, hopefully within a couple of weeks!)

My default plan is to promote 0.1.0-RC2 to 0.1.0 in the next few days, and announce on Monday. (I'll share a draft of the announcement post here for feedback when it's ready, but I have to finish writing the draft first!) So if there are any bugs on RC2 that you can't work around, this will be the last chance to bring them up! :smile:
