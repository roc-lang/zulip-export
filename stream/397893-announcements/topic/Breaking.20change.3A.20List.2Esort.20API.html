Topic: Breaking change: List.sort API

Luke Boswell (Aug 27 2026 at 07:45):

As of this PR https://github.com/roc-lang/roc/pull/9832

  • Replaces the old LT/EQ/GT ordering tags with FirstBeforeSecond, Equivalent, and SecondBeforeFirst
  • Adds default_cmp to built-in types that have a natural default ordering, including numbers and tuples. Strings intentionally do not gain an implicit default ordering.
  • Adds the public list APIs:
    - List.sort
    - List.sort_by
    - List.sort_with
    - List.sort_reversed
    - List.sort_by_reversed
    - List.sort_with_reversed
  • Implements stable List.sort_with as a compiler low-level operation backed by Fluxsort/Quadsort

Thank you to @Jonathan and @Brendan Hansknecht for their work on this.
