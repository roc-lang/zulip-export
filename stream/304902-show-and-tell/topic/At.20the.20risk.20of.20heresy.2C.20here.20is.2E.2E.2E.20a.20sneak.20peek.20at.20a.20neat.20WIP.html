MagicMan (Oct 03 2026 at 19:12):

After re-watching the segment I linked earlier, reading the FAQ, and giving it some thought I am hesitant to be in favour of adding a Lua backend.

I don't think the risk of adding Lua as a compile target is negligible. For one it is a large amount of code to maintain.

More importantly, it might lead to a situation where implementing certain features or optimizations becomes more difficult because it doesn't mesh well with the Lua backend.
This is outlined in the Roc FAQ. https://www.roc-lang.org/faq#other-compilation-targets.
So it may push Roc toward features which it may later come to regret. Please see this, https://youtu.be/WC_j91qogyY?t=2561, segment about Gleam as an example of that.

There's also the question of which version of Lua to support. My understanding is that there are several incompatible dialects, each with their own semantics, features, and bytecode format. http://lua-users.org/wiki/LuaVersionCompatibility

Lastly, Roc already has a great story regarding C FFI and as an embedded scripting language through platforms. https://www.roc-lang.org/docs/main/langref/platforms/#domain-specific-functionality
