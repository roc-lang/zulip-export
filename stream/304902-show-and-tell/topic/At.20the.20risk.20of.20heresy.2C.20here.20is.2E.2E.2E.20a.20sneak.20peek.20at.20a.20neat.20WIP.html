Peter Marreck (Oct 03 2026 at 13:22):

How is LuaJIT better than WASI

Good question. Well for one thing it's battle-tested and already installed in way more places, many of which would surprise you (it did me). LuaJIT has trivial C FFI access, for one thing. It's also more legible to humans than WASI (if you deal with it via WAT). And honestly, part of me was thinking "what's the best first language to teach my son?" and both Roc and Lua were at the top of that list for fairly different, but both legitimate, reasons. ;) ("Learning a language" may be a thing of the past, soon?) But honestly, yeah, WASM is coming in hot here, and I honestly think it's going to sort of be the next LLVM in terms of "popular intermediate build target."

Another thing that might be fun to do is putting a Roc backplane on my Aedicule sideproject
https://github.com/pmarreck/aedicule
The idea behind Aedicule is "universal GUI frontplane" providing drawing/sound/sprite/input primitives, and controls via gpui_component. It's built in Rust and uses GPUI from the Zeditor project. The backplane can be built in anything. Currently it only supports WAT (webassembly/webassembly text) but it's pluggable. I could probably easily add Roc, assuming its wasm output works, and then you'd get the same code running identically as a performant GUI in an app across 3 OS'es as well as web browsers (which is what the handful of existing demos at https://pmarreck.github.io/aedicule/ all already accomplish).
