I made a little game called Dog Park that is mostly in Roc, but also has JS and zig.

Most of the world configuration is in Roc: https://github.com/showell/dog-park/blob/main/roc/World.roc

The basic physics scheme is in Roc as well: https://github.com/showell/dog-park/blob/main/roc/Walk.roc

Without spoiling the game, once you leash a dog, all the nearby critters react to the dog based on their local environment (some critters attract, others repel) and paced by the lead dog's motion.

Play it here: https://dogpark.lynrummy.com/dogpark
