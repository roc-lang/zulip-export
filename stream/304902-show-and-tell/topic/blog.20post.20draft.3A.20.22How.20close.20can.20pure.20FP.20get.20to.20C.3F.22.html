Zak Kohler (Oct 21 2026 at 13:37):

This is kinda a tangent, but I see an oppourtunity to use agent review for continuously auditing against being backed into a performance corner. What I mean is that every feature (including the current set) constrain current, but also _future_ performance improvements or other features. This means that performace regression alone testing can't catch the case of a feature becoming a liability/block because it closes other doors implicitly, sometimes in sneaky/unobvious way.

Perhaps like this:

  1. maintain a list of possible future ideas, including perf. optimizations
  2. nightly review crosschecks the list, spikes/pocs any that might be affected.
  3. Get a report if doors potentially "closed" by recent additions.
