Luke Boswell (Nov 10 2026 at 22:20):

Introducing the latest in our family of packagesroc-docx-- for all your WordML document parsing, reviewing/editing and generation needs.

https://github.com/lukewilliamboswell/roc-docx

It's just an RC for now as I'm still testing it manually and building things with it... but from what I can see so far it feel pretty capable and quite performant.

I asked Claude to use roc-fuzz and maintain various targets to control for correctness and ratchet allocations/performance as we filled out the APIs and that seems to have worked quite well.

Lets me know if you use it or find any rough edges. I plan on building various apps using this for working with business documents.
