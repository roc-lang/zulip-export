Sometimes you just need an escape hatch for making beautiful documents -- well roc-pandoc has you covered!

https://github.com/lukewilliamboswell/roc-pandoc

You can use Roc to author documents via Pandoc's internal IR representation and then run pandoc to generate basically any output format you can dream of.

Some use cases;

  • EBooks
  • PowerPoint slides
  • Word documents
  • Technical Papers
