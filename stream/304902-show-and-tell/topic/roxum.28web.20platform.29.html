It’s been about three months since I started building a platform, and it has finally taken shape, so I’d like to share it here.

I built the web platform:
https://github.com/KouYuume/roxum

I designed it to bring in axum’s intuitive style and its safety, while keeping the friendliness of Roc. Routes and request types are checked at build time, and the compiler keeps domain code pure. Performance is aimed at axum’s level too.
The DDD example is the counterpart of one I wrote in Rust, and it takes less than half the lines.
I hope we can enjoy how simply and functionally Roc lets us write, in web apps too!

I’ve enjoyed many posts on Zulip, and Joy and roux in particular were a great inspiration. I also use roc-fuzz in development. It’s very cool and handy. Thank you!

It doesn’t yet reach axum’s performance, and things like WebSocket aren’t supported yet. I’ll keep developing it, improving it as Roc evolves.

Let’s Roc!!
