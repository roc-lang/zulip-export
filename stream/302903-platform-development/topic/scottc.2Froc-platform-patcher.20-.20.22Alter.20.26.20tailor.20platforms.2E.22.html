"Alter & tailor platforms."

https://github.com/scottc/roc-platform-patcher

So for example... roc-lang/basic-cli@0.26.0

Let's say you wanted to instrument the effects, and have an effects monitoring dashboard... with the ability to observe & alter effects. Or monitor memory allocations etc. Inject any kind of middleware.

Replace effects with noops stubs, crashes, alternative hotswappable dynamic dlopen behaviour.

Or generate mock function calls, fuzzing, in both directions.. towards the app and towards the platform etc.

Or even delete or add platform apis... useful for hardening, or expanding platform functionality, so apps can have access to more or less apis.

Inject dev tooling... display a GUI or websever with various dashboards & tools.

Well now you can do that... automatically & generically across any platforms.

The repo is just a small trivial example of what is possible.

How it works:

roc app/rpp.roc -- \
  --input=GddgvsGjiNXrHFW6au5i5dUDbTs8FinQdrgE2Gqmw53t.tar.zst \
  --output-dir=patched

main! : List(OsStr) => Try({}, _)
main! = |args| {
    config = parse_args(args)

    Stdout.line!("roc-platform-patcher (rpp) v0")?
    Stdout.line!("  input:  ${config.input}")?
    Stdout.line!("  target: ${config.target}")?
    Stdout.line!("  work:   ${config.work_dir}")?
    Stdout.line!("  out:    ${config.output_dir}")?

    unpack!(config)?
    symbols = inspect!(config)?
    rename_originals!(config, symbols)?
    generate_proxies!(config, symbols)?
    rebuild!(config, symbols)?
    write_metadata!(config, symbols)?
    repack!(config)?

    Stdout.line!("")?
    Stdout.line!("Done. Point an app at: ${config.output_dir}/")?
    Stdout.line!("Treat the result as a debug build.")?

    Ok({})
}
