Luke Boswell (Oct 28 2026 at 09:28):

If we assume theyre all equally mature... roc-gui is probably the best choice. Or Karl's gui platform (which he hasnt shared yet :wink:).

I think the gui aspect of roc-ray is better suited for games, though you definitely could.

I think the capabilities oriented security model and builtin testing for roc-gui makes something like this much nicer to work with.

However.... if you wanted to ship it today roc-ray (and terrocotta on top of that) is definitely the most mature.

Luke Boswell (Oct 28 2026 at 09:33):

Regarding the easy path to web... if you are careful about how you structure your app you could probably have two app roots, one could be roc-signals for the web and the other roc-gui. They're both Action-State so the abstractions will be similar.

roc-signals is also experimental.

It kind of comes down to what your goals are here, like learning Roc or platform development.

I call these experimental because making a gui platform is enormous scope and takes a long time to test things properly.
