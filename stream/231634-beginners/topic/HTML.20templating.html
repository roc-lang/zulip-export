Zak Kohler (Nov 04 2026 at 16:50):

If you are looking to make your own server platform, Anton is right, Rocstache templating is tied to the roux platform.

But, if you are looking to make a webapp, consider trying roux. It includes typed db queries+migrations, and fully typed Roc-flavored templates, as well as SSE support. It is tuned for dynamic HTML as well as hypermedia style AJAX e.g. htmx or datastar.

Its super fast: (benchmark) and we have an on boarding tutorial here: https://github.com/Eldhus/roux-tutor
