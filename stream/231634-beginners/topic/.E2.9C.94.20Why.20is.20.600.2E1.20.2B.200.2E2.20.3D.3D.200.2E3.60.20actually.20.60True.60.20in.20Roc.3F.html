Aurélien Geron (Oct 08 2026 at 09:05):

Steve Howell said:

For any math folks out there, I'm kinda curious if F64 closes the door on this. I suspect there are infinite widths where they're equal and infinite widths where they're off by a bit. But I truly don't know.

You successfully nerd-sniped me, I just had to research this. :sweat_smile: My conclusion is that 0.1+0.2 == 0.3 if and only if the number of precision bits is an even number: F32 has 24 precision bits, which is even, so 0.1+0.2 == 0.3. But F64 has 53 bits of precision, which is odd, so 0.1+0.2 != 0.3. Indeed, 0.1, 0.2, and 0.3, all end with repeating 0011 in binary notation. Depending on where you truncate this sequence (rounding the result, with ties go toward even), we get perfect equality or an off-by-one in the last bit.
