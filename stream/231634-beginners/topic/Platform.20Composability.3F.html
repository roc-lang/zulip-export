notramo (Sep 30 2026 at 02:04):

The ideal thing would be that platforms only provide basic functionality like file/socket I/O and concurrency (scheduling, messaging), and everything on top is written in Roc in a platform-agnostic way.

notramo (Sep 30 2026 at 02:04):

If we had a platform agnostic Roc HTTP library, that could be used with a simple socket in basic-cli, but also with any other platform that provides socket primitives.

notramo (Sep 30 2026 at 02:05):

The current basic-webserver does the HTTP in Rust, that's why it's not composable.

notramo (Sep 30 2026 at 02:06):

However, for native Roc features to work, concurrency is required in many cases (for example, to handle multiple requests concurrently in a pure Roc HTTP library). There is no full-featured concurrency in any platform currently.

notramo (Sep 30 2026 at 02:07):

#ideas > Concurrent/threaded platforms

notramo (Sep 30 2026 at 02:08):

It's being explored, but it needs work.

notramo (Sep 30 2026 at 02:10):

Platforms are not just a simple FFI alternative, but much more. Different platforms can implement different styles of I/O, concurrency, memory management, etc.

notramo (Sep 30 2026 at 02:11):

It doesn't help if two platform components are both written in Zig if they use different memory management and I/O practices. It's not only about the language.

notramo (Sep 30 2026 at 02:15):

I think of platforms like if they are like libc for C.

notramo (Sep 30 2026 at 02:16):

Of course, if Roc is used e.g. as an extension language for a game or an editor, the platform has the main functionality, and Roc is just a small part.

notramo (Sep 30 2026 at 02:17):

But an extension environment requires different Roc packages than a whole app or a server.
