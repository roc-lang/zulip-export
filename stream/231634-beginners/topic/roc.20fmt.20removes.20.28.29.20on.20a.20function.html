Aurélien Geron (Oct 01 2026 at 08:59):

You actually need an extra space in this case. This works:

expect [1, 2, 3] |> own_keep(|_| False) .is_empty()

roc fmt will add parentheses to avoid any confusion:

expect ([1, 2, 3] |> own_keep(|_| False)).is_empty()

But you'll often want to define a pipe chain across multiple lines:

expect [1, 2, 3]
    |> own_keep(|_| False)
    .is_empty()
